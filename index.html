Our Story

Love. Passion.
Karma.

Nestled in Arera Colony, Bhopal, Cafe LPK is more than a restaurant — it's a feeling. Perched high above the city skyline, our rooftop venue blends a curated menu, craft cocktails, live performances, and an ambience that turns every evening into an unforgettable story.

From hand-batter pizzas to tandoori delights, cold craft beers to signature cocktails — we have everything you crave. Whether you're here for a casual lunch, a romantic dinner, or a late-night hangout, LPK welcomes you with open arms and warm vibes.
